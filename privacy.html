Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What we collect

  • YouTube links and questions you submit, so we can generate summaries and answers.
  • Support requests: your name, email address and message, delivered to our support inbox.
  • Basic technical data (IP address, browser) used for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Support emails also include the sender's IP address so we can spot spam.

How AI processing works

Video links and questions are sent to the Google Gemini API for processing. Google handles this data under its own terms. On free API tiers, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so please do not submit sensitive personal information.

Storage

Summaries are cached temporarily (up to 24 hours) to speed up repeat requests. Chat history stays in your browser session and is cleared when you close the tab. We do not sell your data.

Email

Support emails are sent through our email provider (Brevo). We use your email address only to reply to your request.

Your rights

To have your support correspondence deleted, contact us through the Support page.
